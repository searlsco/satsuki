## Upstream: Tsutsuji

Pattern identifiers (`l2_id`), surface forms, readings, class codes/names,
difficulty, register, and attachment rules referenced by this corpus come
from the Tsutsuji Japanese functional expression dictionary:

- 松吉俊・佐藤理史・宇津呂武仁. 日本語機能表現辞書「つつじ」
- <https://sites.google.com/edu.teu.ac.jp/cl-lab/研究/言語資源/日本語機能表現辞書つつじ>
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0

The glosses, usage notes, and example sentences in `data/` are original to
this corpus; Tsutsuji itself deliberately contains no meanings, English, or
examples.
